How the diameter is found: from the distance of each point to the selected axis — the main value is the 99th percentile (rejects outliers), while "max ⌀" is the farthest point. Reliable for cylindrical parts; for irregular geometry rely on the bounding-box dimensions.
Unit note: STL files are unitless. Values are read without scaling and assumed to be in mm (the CAD convention). If your model was built in cm/inch, convert the results accordingly.

Measurements are for reference · rely on the original CAD/drawing for manufacturing tolerances
